TESS Planet Hunter

A bot that wakes up every few hours, pulls light curves for stars NASA's TESS satellite observed but never examined closely, searches them for the tiny periodic dimming of a transiting planet, and ranks every signal with a machine-learning vetter. Everything below was found automatically.

How it works

01
Select
Random sky fields; FGK/M dwarfs, Tmag 9–13, low contamination, from the TESS Input Catalog.
02
Clean
Download SPOC/QLP light curves from MAST, remove flares, divide out stellar variability.
03
Search
Box Least Squares over 0.5–15 day periods, iterative to catch multi-planet systems.
04
Vet
Odd/even depth, secondary eclipse, shape, radius, duration physics, then an AI score.
05
Cross-match
Against every TOI, confirmed planet and TESS eclipsing binary to isolate what is new.
